Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

Resolving Disputes. Protecting Interests.

Baba Law Firm advises and represents domestic and international clients in commercial disputes, arbitration proceedings and litigation before the Syrian courts.

We assist businesses, investors and organisations in assessing disputes, developing legal strategies and pursuing appropriate methods of resolution, whether through negotiation, arbitration or court proceedings.

Our dispute resolution practice is informed by the firm’s long-standing experience in arbitration, administrative law and the Syrian judiciary.

Arbitration and the Syrian Courts

Judicial Support and Supervision.

The relationship between arbitral tribunals and national courts is an area of particular experience and academic focus within the firm.

Our experience in this field draws on the judicial and arbitral career of Senior Counsel Tawfeeq Baba, former President of the Syrian Council of State, who has served as presiding arbitrator in numerous disputes involving foreign companies and Syrian government entities.

It is further informed by the doctoral research of Managing Partner Dr. Mohamad Baba, whose dissertation, The Role of National Courts in Arbitration Matters, examines the relationship between arbitration and national judicial authorities, and whose earlier research addressed arbitration in administrative contracts under Syrian law.

Courts may be called upon to support the arbitral process, rule on the validity of arbitration agreements, grant interim measures, or determine applications to set aside or enforce arbitral awards.

We assist clients in navigating the relationship between arbitration and court proceedings before, during and after arbitration.

Arbitration and Dispute Resolution in Syria

Understanding the Legal Framework.

Arbitration in Syria is principally governed by Arbitration Law No. 4 of 2008, which establishes the legal framework for arbitration agreements, the conduct of arbitral proceedings and procedures relating to arbitral awards. Syria is also a party to the 1958 New York Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards, and the enforcement of foreign awards is subject to the applicable treaty provisions and procedural requirements under Syrian law.

Arbitration involving administrative contracts is subject to a distinct legal framework, including Article 66 of Public Contracts Law No. 51 of 2004 and Article 10 of Council of State Law No. 32 of 2019.

Commercial and civil litigation is governed by the relevant substantive laws and procedural rules, including the Code of Civil Procedure (Law No. 1 of 2016).

We advise clients on selecting appropriate dispute resolution mechanisms, assessing jurisdictional and procedural requirements, and pursuing the legal remedies available under Syrian law.

How We Assist

Our work includes:

  • Domestic and International Arbitration — Advising and representing clients in commercial arbitration proceedings involving Syrian law, Syrian parties or transactions connected with Syria.
  • Arbitration Involving Public Entities — Advising on arbitration arising from administrative contracts and agreements with Syrian public entities, including the specific legal requirements applicable to such arbitration and the jurisdiction of the competent courts.
  • Arbitration Agreements and Clauses — Drafting, reviewing and advising on arbitration clauses and agreements, including their validity, scope, governing law and procedural implications.
  • Court Proceedings Related to Arbitration — Advising and representing clients in judicial proceedings concerning arbitration agreements, the appointment or challenge of arbitrators, interim measures and other matters involving judicial assistance or supervision.
  • Challenges to Arbitral Awards — Advising on applications to set aside arbitral awards and representing clients in related proceedings before the competent Syrian courts.
  • Recognition and Enforcement of Arbitral Awards — Advising and representing clients in proceedings concerning the recognition and enforcement of domestic and foreign arbitral awards in Syria.
  • Commercial and Civil Litigation — Representing businesses and individuals in contractual, commercial, corporate and civil disputes before the competent Syrian courts.
  • Contractual and Construction Disputes — Advising on disputes arising from commercial agreements, construction contracts, supply arrangements, project performance, delays and contractual termination.
  • Shareholder and Corporate Disputes — Advising and representing clients in disputes involving shareholders, company management, ownership interests and corporate obligations.
  • Negotiation and Amicable Settlement — Assisting clients in negotiating settlements, documenting settlement arrangements and resolving disputes without formal proceedings where appropriate.
  • Enforcement of Judgments and Debt Recovery — Advising and representing clients in proceedings for the enforcement of court judgments and recovery of outstanding contractual and commercial debts.

Commercial Litigation and Dispute Strategy

From Initial Assessment to Resolution.

Disputes may arise from the interpretation or performance of contracts, unpaid obligations, changes in business relationships, corporate disagreements or the termination of commercial arrangements.

An effective legal strategy requires consideration of the available evidence, the contractual framework, the applicable law, jurisdictional questions and the practical prospects of enforcement.

We assist clients from the initial assessment of a dispute through negotiations, proceedings and enforcement, with attention to the legal and commercial consequences of each available course of action.
