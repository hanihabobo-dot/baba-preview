What Happens Next

From Enquiry to Engagement.

  1. 01Review — We review your enquiry and, where appropriate, conduct a preliminary conflicts check before discussing the matter in detail.
  2. 02Initial discussion — We contact you to understand your objectives, the background of the matter and the advice you require.
  3. 03Engagement — If we are able to act, we confirm in writing the scope of our work, the team responsible and the terms of engagement before work begins.
