## Foreign Investment in Syria
~ Understanding the Investment Framework.
Foreign investment in Syria is governed by a combination of investment legislation, company law and sector-specific regulations.
Investment Law No. 18 of 2021, as amended, including by Decree No. 114 of 2025, establishes an important legal framework for qualifying investment projects, including provisions relating to investment incentives, guarantees and applicable administrative procedures.
Not all business activities fall within the scope of the Investment Law. Depending on the proposed activity, an investor may establish a company under the Syrian Companies Law (Legislative Decree No. 29 of 2011, as amended), register a branch or representative office under Law No. 34 of 2008, appoint a registered Syrian commercial agent or distributor, or pursue an investment project subject to specific statutory requirements.
Licensing conditions, minimum capital requirements and regulatory approvals may differ according to the nature of the business and the sector in which it operates.
We advise clients on the legal framework applicable to their proposed investments, including the distinction between ordinary commercial activities and projects eligible for treatment under investment legislation.

## Key Considerations for Foreign Investors
### Transfer of Funds.
Investment projects licensed under the Investment Law benefit from statutory guarantees, including the transfer of profits abroad, the repatriation of foreign capital in specified circumstances and the transfer of compensation in a convertible currency, subject to applicable statutory conditions and banking regulations. Non-Syrian employees of such projects may also transfer their wages abroad through banks operating in Syria. Businesses established outside the Investment Law framework remain subject to the general foreign exchange regulations of the Central Bank of Syria. We advise investors on the transfer rights available to them and on structuring investments to secure those rights.

### Real Estate and Land.
Real estate ownership by non-Syrians is generally regulated by Law No. 11 of 2011, as amended, which imposes restrictions and approval requirements. Investment projects may benefit from specific provisions under Investment Law No. 18 of 2021, as amended, concerning the acquisition or leasing of property required for their activities. State-owned land may also be allocated to investors in accordance with the applicable investment legislation. We advise on the legal requirements governing land acquisition, leasing and allocation for investment projects.
### International Compliance.
International economic sanctions on Syria have been lifted, opening the market to foreign investment and international partnerships. Investors should nevertheless ensure that their counterparties, financing and banking arrangements meet the compliance requirements of the jurisdictions in which they and their partners operate. We assist investors in assessing these requirements in coordination with their international advisers.

## Establishing a Presence in Syria
~ From Initial Assessment to Operations.
Certain investments may qualify for procedures or incentives under investment legislation, while others are governed primarily by company law and sector-specific licensing requirements.
In Syria, foreign investors may need to address several legal and administrative requirements before commencing operations, particularly where the proposed activity involves regulated sectors, industrial facilities, infrastructure or dealings with public authorities.
We assist clients from the initial legal assessment of a proposed investment through establishment, licensing and the commencement of operations.
