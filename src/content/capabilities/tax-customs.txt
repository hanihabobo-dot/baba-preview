## Taxation in Syria
~ Understanding Fiscal Obligations and Managing Legal Exposure.
The Syrian tax framework is governed by several legislative instruments, including Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2003, as amended, together with the applicable legislation governing consumption expenditure duties, stamp duties, property-related taxes and other fiscal obligations. The tax framework is the subject of ongoing reform, and the applicable rules should be confirmed in light of current legislation for each transaction.
The tax treatment of a business depends on its legal structure, activities, sources of income and the specific provisions applicable to the relevant taxpayer or transaction.
Businesses operating in Syria may be subject to corporate income tax, taxes on salaries and wages, withholding obligations, consumption expenditure duties and other applicable taxes and fees. Particular considerations may arise in transactions involving non-resident persons, foreign contractors, cross-border services and payments to parties outside Syria.
Compliance obligations may include tax registration, the maintenance of supporting documentation, the submission of declarations and responses to assessments or inquiries by the competent financial authorities.
We advise clients on the interpretation and application of Syrian tax legislation, the fiscal consequences of commercial arrangements and the legal procedures available for addressing tax assessments and disputes.

## Customs Regulation in Syria
~ Supporting International Trade and Customs Compliance.
The customs framework in Syria is governed by Customs Decree No. 109 of 2026, which replaced Customs Laws Nos. 37 and 38 of 2006, as amended, together with the Harmonized Customs Tariff adopted under Decree No. 110 of 2026, as amended by Decree No. 188 of 2026, and the applicable implementing regulations and administrative decisions. Customs administration falls under the General Authority for Borders and Customs.

The customs regime governs the importation, exportation, transit and storage of goods, as well as customs declarations, tariff classification, valuation, rules of origin, exemptions, customs guarantees and the obligations of parties involved in cross-border trade.
The duties and procedures applicable to a particular shipment depend on the nature and classification of the goods, their customs value, origin, intended use and the customs procedure under which they are declared.
Businesses may also be subject to additional licensing, inspection, certification or regulatory requirements depending on the products involved and the applicable import or export restrictions.
We assist clients in interpreting customs legislation, assessing customs-related liabilities, addressing regulatory issues affecting the movement of goods and managing disputes with the competent customs authorities.

## Tax and Customs Considerations for Foreign Investors
~ Assessing the Fiscal Framework for Investment.
Foreign investment in Syria may give rise to tax and customs considerations at the stages of business establishment, capital investment, importation of equipment, commencement of operations and the distribution or transfer of profits. Investment Law No. 18 of 2021, as amended, provides for incentives and exemptions for qualifying projects, subject to the conditions, approvals and requirements established by law.
We assist investors in evaluating the legal requirements governing investment-related tax and customs benefits, reviewing contractual and regulatory arrangements and identifying fiscal risks associated with proposed projects.
