## Government Regulation and Licensing in Syria
~ Managing Regulatory Requirements and Administrative Approvals.
Businesses operating in Syria may require licences, permits, registrations or other approvals from ministries, regulatory bodies and competent administrative authorities.
The requirements applicable to a particular activity depend on its sector, the legal status of the business and the legislation governing the relevant authority. Regulated activities may involve preliminary authorisations, technical conditions, operational requirements and continuing compliance obligations.
Administrative approvals may also be subject to conditions, renewal requirements or amendment procedures following changes in business activities, ownership or management.
We assist clients in identifying applicable regulatory requirements, preparing and reviewing submissions, addressing legal issues arising during approval procedures and managing their ongoing obligations before public authorities.

## Public Procurement and Government Contracts
~ Understanding Procurement Procedures and Contractual Obligations.
Public procurement in Syria is principally governed by Public Contracts Law No. 51 of 2004, as amended, together with its implementing regulations and any special rules applicable to particular public entities or transactions.
The legal framework regulates the methods through which public authorities procure works, supplies and services, including public tenders and other contracting procedures permitted by law.

Participation in public procurement requires careful consideration of eligibility and qualification conditions, tender specifications, technical and financial submissions, bid securities, evaluation procedures and the requirements governing contract awards.
Government contracts also raise legal questions concerning the authority of the contracting entity, the approvals required for the proposed transaction and the formalities governing the conclusion and effectiveness of the contract.
Once awarded, public contracts may involve specific obligations relating to performance guarantees, payment, variations, price adjustments, delays, extensions of time, penalties, termination and the settlement of contractual claims.
We advise clients throughout the procurement and contracting process, from reviewing tender documents and assessing contractual risks to negotiating terms, managing performance-related issues and pursuing available remedies.

## Administrative Contracts and Dispute Resolution
~ Understanding Public Law Obligations and Available Remedies.
Contracts concluded with public authorities may qualify as administrative contracts depending on their nature, their connection with public services and the applicable legal framework. The involvement of a public entity does not, by itself, determine the legal character of a contract.
Administrative contracts are subject to a distinct legal regime, and disputes arising from them generally fall within the jurisdiction of the Council of State, subject to the applicable rules governing jurisdiction and dispute resolution.
Arbitration in administrative contracts is subject to specific statutory requirements, including Article 66 of Public Contracts Law No. 51 of 2004 and Article 10 of Council of State Law No. 32 of 2019. The validity and enforceability of an arbitration agreement must be assessed in light of the approvals and conditions required by the applicable legislation.
We assist clients in evaluating the legal character of their contracts, identifying the appropriate dispute resolution mechanism, assessing contractual claims and pursuing available remedies before the competent courts or arbitral tribunals.
