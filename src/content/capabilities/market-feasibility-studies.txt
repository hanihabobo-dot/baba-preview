# Market & Feasibility Studies
~ Market Insight. Legal Clarity.
Baba Law Firm prepares market studies, feasibility studies and investment potential assessments for domestic and international investors, companies and institutions considering projects or business activities in Syria.
What distinguishes our studies is that the market and financial analysis is combined with a detailed review of the legal and regulatory framework that applies to the proposed activity. Clients receive an integrated assessment in which commercial and financial conclusions take account of the legal and regulatory requirements applicable to the proposed project.

## Assessing Opportunities in Syria
~ Market Conditions and the Legal Framework.
As Syria’s economy reopens to international trade and investment, investors are evaluating opportunities across a wide range of sectors. A reliable assessment of these opportunities requires an understanding of demand, competition, costs and market practices, as well as the licensing requirements, ownership rules, incentives and regulatory obligations that apply to each activity.
In Syria, legal and regulatory requirements can have a direct effect on whether a project is viable and how it should be structured. Sector-specific licensing, restrictions on foreign ownership, eligibility for investment incentives, tax and customs treatment, and access to land and infrastructure may all affect the cost, timetable and risk of an investment.

## Why a Legal Perspective Matters
~ Commercial Conclusions Grounded in the Law.
A market or feasibility study is only as reliable as its assumptions. In Syria, many of the most important assumptions are legal: whether the activity can be licensed, whether foreign ownership is permitted, which incentives and exemptions are available, how the project will be taxed, and how land, imports and financing can be secured.
Because our studies are prepared by a law firm, these questions are analysed as part of the study itself, rather than left as assumptions to be confirmed at a later stage.
The same team can then advise on structuring, licensing and implementing the project, so that the conclusions of the study and the legal work that follows rest on the same analysis.

## How We Assist
Our work includes:
- **Market Studies** — Assessing demand, competition, pricing, distribution channels and the principal commercial factors affecting a proposed product, service or project in Syria.
- **Feasibility Studies** — Preparing feasibility studies covering the market, financial and legal aspects of proposed projects, including cost estimates, revenue projections and sensitivity analysis.
- **Investment Potential Assessments** — Evaluating the investment potential of sectors, locations and specific opportunities in Syria, including the regulatory environment, available incentives and principal risks.
- **Legal and Regulatory Feasibility** — Identifying the licences, approvals, ownership requirements and regulatory conditions that apply to a proposed activity, and assessing their effect on the structure, timetable and cost of the project.
- **Investment Incentives and Structuring Options** — Assessing eligibility for treatment under Investment Law No. 18 of 2021, as amended by Law No. 2 of 2023 and Decree No. 114 of 2025, and comparing the investment and corporate structures available to the investor.
- **Risk Assessment** — Identifying the legal, regulatory, commercial and operational risks associated with a proposed investment and the measures available to manage them.
- **Studies for Financing, Partners and Approvals** — Preparing studies to support financing applications, investment discussions, partnership arrangements and regulatory submissions, subject to the requirements of the relevant institution or authority.
