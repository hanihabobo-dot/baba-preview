## Banking and Financial Regulation in Syria
~ Understanding the Legal Framework.
Banking and financial activities in Syria operate within a regulatory framework established principally by the Law on the Central Bank of Syria and the Basic Monetary System (Law No. 23 of 2002, as amended), the legislation governing private banks (Law No. 28 of 2001, as amended) and Islamic banks (Legislative Decree No. 35 of 2005, as amended), together with the regulations and decisions of the Central Bank of Syria and the Council of Money and Credit.
The Central Bank of Syria plays a central role in the regulation and supervision of banking activities, while specific legal requirements apply to banks, financial institutions and other regulated financial service providers.
Depending on the nature of the transaction, relevant legal considerations may include licensing and regulatory approvals, lending restrictions, foreign exchange regulations, anti-money laundering obligations, financial guarantees and the enforceability of contractual arrangements.
We advise clients on the application of Syrian banking and financial legislation to their operations and transactions, including the legal requirements applicable to financing, security arrangements and regulatory compliance.

## Financing and Security Arrangements
~ Protecting Financial and Commercial Interests.
Financing transactions require careful attention to the rights and obligations of lenders, borrowers and guarantors.
Under Syrian law, the effectiveness of a financing arrangement may depend on the proper documentation of contractual obligations, the legal capacity and authority of the parties, and compliance with requirements governing guarantees and security interests.
Particular consideration may be necessary where financing is secured by real estate, movable assets, shares or other property rights.
We assist clients in structuring financing arrangements, reviewing the enforceability of security documents and addressing legal issues that may arise during repayment, restructuring or enforcement.

## Cross-Border Banking and Financial Transactions
~ Navigating Regulatory and Contractual Requirements.
International financing and payment arrangements involving Syria may require consideration of both Syrian legal requirements and the regulatory frameworks applicable to foreign financial institutions.

Issues relating to currency transfers, banking channels, the compliance requirements of foreign financial institutions and the recognition or enforcement of financial obligations can affect the structure and implementation of cross-border transactions.
We advise clients on the Syrian legal aspects of international banking and financing arrangements and coordinate, where necessary, with foreign legal and financial advisers.
