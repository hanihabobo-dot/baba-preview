## Construction and Infrastructure Projects in Syria
~ Understanding the Legal and Contractual Framework.
Construction and infrastructure projects in Syria operate within a legal framework that includes civil and commercial law, construction regulations, public contracting legislation and sector-specific requirements.
The Syrian Civil Code (Legislative Decree No. 84 of 1949) governs fundamental contractual obligations and construction-related liabilities, including provisions applicable to contracts for works, the responsibilities of contractors and engineers, and their decennial liability for structural defects affecting the stability or safety of buildings and other fixed structures.
Projects involving public authorities and state-owned entities may be subject to Public Contracts Law No. 51 of 2004, as amended, together with applicable regulations governing public procurement, contract performance and administrative obligations.
Infrastructure projects may also be structured under the Public-Private Partnership Law (Law No. 5 of 2016), as amended, where applicable, or through other legally permitted project arrangements. Investment guarantees under Investment Law No. 18 of 2021, as amended by Decree No. 114 of 2025, expressly extend to projects implemented under BOT, BOO and PPP models.
Depending on the nature of the project, additional requirements may arise in relation to construction permits, engineering approvals, contractor classification, environmental obligations, land use and sector-specific authorisations.
International construction projects may also involve contractual arrangements based on internationally recognised standard forms, including FIDIC contracts, adapted to the requirements of Syrian law and the circumstances of the project.
We advise clients on the legal framework applicable to their projects, including the distinction between private construction arrangements and contracts involving public entities.

## Public-Sector Projects and Administrative Contracts
~ Addressing the Requirements of Government Projects.
Infrastructure and construction projects involving Syrian public authorities may be governed by legal and contractual requirements that differ from those applicable to private-sector projects.
Public-sector contracts may involve specific procedures concerning tendering, contract award, performance guarantees, price adjustments, variations, delays, termination and dispute resolution.

The legal characterisation of a contract and the status of the contracting authority may affect the applicable rules and the jurisdiction of the competent courts.
We advise clients on the contractual and administrative requirements associated with public-sector projects, including the legal implications of project modifications, performance difficulties and disputes.
