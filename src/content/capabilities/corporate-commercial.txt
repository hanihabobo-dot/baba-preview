## Commercial Law in Syria
~ Understanding the Legal Framework.
Commercial relationships in Syria are principally governed by the Civil Code (Legislative Decree No. 84 of 1949) and the Commercial Code (Law No. 33 of 2007).
Commercial agency and distribution arrangements are also subject to specific legal requirements, including those established under Law No. 34 of 2008 concerning the registration of foreign businesses, commercial agents and intermediaries.
Depending on the nature of the transaction, additional requirements may also arise in relation to import and export activities, foreign exchange, regulatory approvals and dealings with public entities.
We advise clients on how Syrian law applies to their commercial arrangements, including the enforceability of contractual provisions, the allocation of liability and the legal consequences of non-performance. For international businesses, we also address how Syrian legal requirements interact with cross-border commercial practice.

## Supporting Commercial Relationships
~ From Negotiation to Performance.
Commercial agreements must be legally sound and capable of functioning effectively in practice.
For businesses operating in Syria, provisions concerning payment, delivery, liability, termination and dispute resolution require careful consideration of the applicable legal framework and the practical circumstances of performance.
We assist clients throughout the contractual relationship, from initial negotiations and drafting to implementation, amendment and the resolution of contractual difficulties.
