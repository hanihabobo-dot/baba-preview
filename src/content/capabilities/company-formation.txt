# Company Formation & Corporate Structuring
~ Establishing Businesses. Structuring for Growth.
Establishing a business in Syria requires careful consideration of its legal structure, regulatory obligations and long-term commercial needs.
Baba Law Firm advises domestic and international clients on company formation and corporate structuring, assisting with incorporation, ownership and management arrangements, and the legal requirements of operating businesses in Syria.

## The Legal Framework
~ Choosing the Right Structure.
The choice of legal structure can affect ownership rights, management authority, liability, regulatory obligations and future business development.
Syrian law provides for several forms of business organisation, including limited liability companies, joint-stock companies, general partnerships and limited partnerships. Sole proprietorships may also be appropriate for certain business activities.
Company formation is principally governed by the Syrian Companies Law, Legislative Decree No. 29 of 2011, as amended.
Investment projects may also be subject to Investment Law No. 18 of 2021, as amended, depending on the nature of the investment and the applicable legal requirements.
We advise clients on the legal implications of available structures, including registration with the Commercial Register, ownership arrangements, management authority and ongoing corporate obligations.
