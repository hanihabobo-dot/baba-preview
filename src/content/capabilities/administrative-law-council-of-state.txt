## Administrative Law and Judicial Review in Syria
~ Understanding the Legal Limits of Public Authority.
Administrative law governs the exercise of public powers and the legal relationships between administrative authorities and the individuals or entities affected by their decisions.
In Syria, Council of State Law No. 32 of 2019 establishes the institutional and judicial framework governing the Council of State, including its jurisdiction over administrative disputes as prescribed by law.
Administrative decisions may affect business licences, investment activities, property interests, public employment, regulatory approvals and other legally protected rights.
We advise clients on the legal consequences of administrative decisions, the grounds on which they may be challenged and the remedies available under Syrian administrative law.

## Challenging Administrative Decisions
~ Annulment, Compensation and Judicial Protection.
Administrative decisions may be challenged where they are affected by defects of competence, form or procedure, violation of the law, errors concerning their legal or factual basis, or misuse of administrative powers.
An action for annulment requires careful consideration of the nature of the contested measure, the claimant’s legal interest, the competent court and the applicable conditions of admissibility.
Administrative grievances, filing deadlines and procedural requirements may affect the availability of judicial remedies. Where the law permits, an application for suspension of execution may also be considered to address the consequences of a contested decision pending judicial determination.

Unlawful administrative action may additionally give rise to compensation claims where the applicable legal requirements are satisfied.
We assist clients in evaluating administrative decisions, preparing grievances and judicial challenges, and pursuing annulment, compensation or other remedies before the competent authorities and courts.

## Council of State Jurisdiction and Procedure
~ Identifying the Appropriate Forum and Legal Remedy.
Administrative disputes are governed by rules of jurisdiction and procedure that differ from those applicable to ordinary civil and commercial litigation.
Determining the appropriate forum requires an assessment of the nature of the contested administrative act or legal relationship, the powers exercised by the public authority and the statutory provisions governing the dispute.
Not every dispute involving a government entity falls within the jurisdiction of the Council of State. Certain matters may be assigned to the ordinary courts or other competent bodies under applicable legislation.
For foreign companies operating through a registered branch or office in Syria, Article 60 of Law No. 34 of 2008 provides that disputes between the branch or office and a Syrian public entity with which it has contracted fall within the jurisdiction of the administrative judiciary, while disputes with other contracting parties are heard by the civil courts of first instance.
Proceedings before the administrative judiciary may involve specific requirements concerning standing, prior grievances, filing periods, admissibility, evidence and the scope of available remedies.
We assist clients in identifying the competent forum, assessing procedural requirements and developing litigation strategies appropriate to the legal and factual circumstances of each case.
