## Governance Under Syrian Law
~ Defining Responsibilities. Managing Legal Risk.
Corporate governance in Syria is shaped by the legal form of the company, its constitutional documents and the applicable provisions of the Syrian Companies Law, Legislative Decree No. 29 of 2011, as amended.
Governance requirements differ according to whether a business operates as a limited liability company, joint-stock company or another recognised corporate form.
We advise clients on the allocation of authority between shareholders, general assemblies, boards of directors and company managers, as applicable.
Our work addresses voting rights, representation and signing powers, management responsibilities, corporate approvals and the procedures required for legally effective decisions.
For companies with foreign shareholders or cross-border management arrangements, we also advise on adapting governance structures to Syrian legal requirements while taking account of international business practices.

## Governance in Practice
~ Supporting Decisions That Stand on a Sound Legal Basis.
Governance arrangements must work not only on paper but also in the day-to-day management of a business.
In Syria, questions concerning who may represent a company, sign contracts, authorise payments or approve significant transactions can have important legal and commercial consequences.
We help clients establish clear procedures for corporate decision-making and ensure that management authority is appropriately reflected in the company’s legal documents and records.
Where disputes arise between shareholders or questions emerge regarding the validity of corporate decisions, we advise on the available legal options and appropriate procedures.
