## Mergers and Acquisitions in Syria
~ Understanding the Legal and Regulatory Framework.
Mergers, acquisitions and corporate restructuring in Syria require consideration of the company’s legal form, ownership structure, constitutional documents and applicable regulatory requirements.
The Syrian Companies Law (Legislative Decree No. 29 of 2011, as amended) provides the principal legal framework for mergers, corporate reorganisations and changes in company ownership and capital.
Depending on the transaction, additional requirements may arise in relation to merger control under the Competition and Anti-Monopoly Law (Law No. 7 of 2008), foreign ownership, sector-specific approvals, commercial registration, taxation, employment obligations and the transfer of assets or contractual rights.
Transactions involving banks, insurers, listed companies or other regulated businesses may also require approvals or notifications from the relevant supervisory authorities.
We advise clients on the legal requirements applicable to their proposed transactions, including the procedures necessary to implement ownership changes, corporate reorganisations and related amendments to company documents and registrations.

## Legal Due Diligence
~ Identifying Risks Before Commitment.
A proposed acquisition or investment requires an understanding of the legal position of the business concerned.
In Syria, due diligence may involve reviewing the company’s registration and ownership records, the authority of its representatives, material contracts, existing liabilities, regulatory licences and pending disputes.
Particular attention may be required where a transaction involves real estate, public-sector contracts, regulated activities or foreign shareholders.
We assist clients in identifying legal risks that may affect transaction value, contractual protections or the ability to complete the proposed acquisition.
Our findings inform the negotiation of transaction documents, including representations, warranties, indemnities and conditions to completion.
