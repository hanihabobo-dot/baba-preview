## A Market Opening to Investment
~ Local Understanding for International Business.
As Syria’s economy reopens to international trade and investment, opportunities require careful assessment of the legal framework, regulatory requirements and practical conditions for doing business.
We help clients evaluate their options, establish an appropriate presence, structure investments and commercial relationships, and address the legal issues that arise as their activities develop.

## How We Help
~ Legal Support at Every Stage.
- **Entering the Market** — Preparing market and feasibility studies, assessing market-entry options, establishing companies and foreign branches, structuring ownership and obtaining licences and regulatory approvals.
- **Operating in Syria** — Supporting commercial contracts, corporate governance, employment, banking arrangements, taxation and ongoing regulatory compliance.
- **Investing and Growing** — Structuring acquisitions, joint ventures, financing, real estate developments, and construction and infrastructure projects.

- **Resolving Disputes** — Protecting clients’ interests through negotiation, arbitration, commercial litigation, enforcement and proceedings before the Syrian Council of State.
