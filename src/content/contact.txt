## What Happens Next
~ From Enquiry to Engagement.
- **Review** — We review your enquiry and, where appropriate, conduct a preliminary conflicts check before discussing the matter in detail.
- **Initial discussion** — We contact you to understand your objectives, the background of the matter and the advice you require.
- **Engagement** — If we are able to act, we confirm in writing the scope of our work, the team responsible and the terms of engagement before work begins.
