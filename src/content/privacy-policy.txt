# Privacy Policy
~ Respecting Privacy. Protecting Information.
Effective from the date this website is first published.
Baba Law Firm (the “Firm”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, contact us, seek legal advice or apply for employment. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, retain and protect personal information in connection with our website and related communications.

## 1. Who We Are
Baba Law Firm is an independent law firm based in Damascus, Syria (Mazzeh Highway, Al-Iskan Building, 8th Floor), and is responsible for the personal information processed in connection with this website and our communications. For questions concerning this Policy or the handling of your personal information, contact us at info@babalawfirm.net or through the Contact page.

## 2. Information We Collect
Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect:
- Contact information, including your name, organisation, position, email address and telephone number.
- Information you provide in enquiries, correspondence, meeting requests and instructions for legal services.
- Information relevant to a prospective or existing professional engagement, including corporate, transactional or case-related records.
- Recruitment information, including CVs, qualifications, professional experience and application correspondence.
- Technical information associated with website use, such as IP address, browser type, device information and security logs; and cookie or analytics data where enabled.
Please avoid sending sensitive or confidential information through the general website contact form before an engagement has been agreed.

## 3. How We Use Information
We use personal information to respond to enquiries, assess potential engagements, provide legal services, communicate with clients and counterparties, administer recruitment, maintain website security, comply with applicable legal and professional obligations, and improve our website where permitted.

## 4. Legal Grounds and Professional Obligations
We process information in accordance with applicable law, including Law No. 12 of 2024 on the Protection of Electronic Personal Data, where necessary to respond to a request, take steps towards or perform a professional engagement, meet legal obligations, protect legitimate business and security interests, or where valid consent is required and obtained. The appropriate legal ground may depend on the individual and the relevant jurisdiction.

## 5. Sharing and International Processing
Information may be shared with authorised members of our team, professional advisers, service providers supporting website hosting, communications or security, and competent authorities where legally required. In connection with cross-border matters, information may be shared with relevant foreign advisers or service providers where appropriate and legally permissible. We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Security and Confidentiality
We take reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse and disclosure. Information relating to legal matters is also handled in accordance with applicable professional duties of confidentiality. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Retention
We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to applicable legal, professional and record-keeping requirements. Applications for advertised positions are normally retained for up to 90 days after the relevant recruitment process, and general applications for up to 90 days from the date of receipt, unless a longer period is required by law or separately agreed with the applicant.

## 8. Your Requests and Choices
Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, or raise concerns about its processing. Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw that consent as permitted by law. We will consider requests in light of legal and professional obligations, including confidentiality and record-retention requirements.

## 9. Cookies and External Services
Our website may use essential cookies and, subject to the settings implemented on the site, analytics or other optional technologies. Further information is provided in our Cookie Policy. Links to third-party websites are governed by those websites’ own privacy practices.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Policy to reflect changes in our practices, website functionality or applicable requirements. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page.
