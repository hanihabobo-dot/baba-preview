# Tawfeeq Baba
**Senior Counsel** \\ Former President of the Syrian Council of State

## Profile
Tawfeeq Baba is Senior Counsel at Baba Law Firm, bringing extensive experience from a distinguished judicial career that culminated in nine years as President of the Syrian Council of State and the Supreme Administrative Court.
He founded Baba Law Firm in Aleppo in 1967 before joining the judiciary. In Aleppo, he served as an investigating judge, indictment judge and Chief Public Prosecutor, and later headed the Judicial Inspection Department for the Northern Region.
In 2002, he was appointed to the Syrian Court of Cassation as Presiding Judge of its First Criminal Chamber. In 2003, he became President of the Syrian Council of State and the Supreme Administrative Court, serving in both capacities until 2012.
His professional experience also includes serving as presiding arbitrator and chair of arbitral tribunals in numerous commercial disputes involving foreign companies, particularly those arising from supply agreements and contracts with Syrian government entities.
Following his retirement from the judiciary in 2012, he returned to the firm he founded, where he continues to contribute his judicial and arbitral experience as Senior Counsel.
