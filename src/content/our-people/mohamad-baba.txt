# Dr. Mohamad Baba
**Managing Partner** \\ PhD in Private Law | LL.M., Fordham University School of Law

## Profile
Dr. Mohamad Baba is Managing Partner of Baba Law Firm, where he leads the firm’s corporate, commercial and dispute resolution practice. He advises domestic and international clients on corporate and commercial law, foreign investment, banking and finance, corporate governance and arbitration.
He advises businesses and investors on establishing and operating in Syria. His work covers company formation and corporate structuring, commercial transactions, investment requirements and regulatory compliance, as well as legal and administrative procedures and the management of legal risks.
His dispute resolution practice includes commercial litigation, arbitration and matters involving public authorities. Throughout his work, he combines a close understanding of Syrian law and legal institutions with the commercial priorities of domestic and international clients.
His practice is informed by advanced academic research in private law, with a particular focus on arbitration, administrative contracts and the relationship between arbitral proceedings and national courts.
