# Careers
~ Build Your Career. Develop Your Expertise.
At Baba Law Firm, we value sound legal judgment, professional integrity, intellectual curiosity and a commitment to client service.
Our practice brings together lawyers working across corporate and commercial law, foreign investment, banking and finance, regulatory matters, arbitration, litigation and administrative law. We welcome interest from professionals who wish to contribute to this work.

## Working at Baba Law Firm
~ Professional Development. Collaborative Practice.
Our lawyers work on matters involving Syrian law, local institutions and the commercial needs of domestic and international clients. We value careful research, clear drafting, discretion, teamwork and the ability to translate legal analysis into practical advice.

## Who We Welcome
We welcome expressions of interest from qualified lawyers, junior associates, trainees and other legal professionals whose skills and experience align with our practice. Fluency in Arabic is important for many roles; strong English or French language skills may be advantageous, depending on the position.

## What We Look For
~ Skills That Serve Our Clients.
- **Legal analysis** — Sound knowledge of Syrian law and the ability to apply it to practical problems.
- **Drafting** — Clear and accurate drafting in Arabic and, for many roles, in English.
- **Professional conduct** — Integrity, discretion and respect for client confidentiality.
- **Teamwork** — The ability to work effectively with colleagues, clients and advisers in other jurisdictions.

## Apply to Join Us
~ Send Your CV.
To express your interest in joining Baba Law Firm, please send your CV and a brief covering message to careers@babalawfirm.net, indicating the position or area of practice in which you are interested.
-> Email Your CV →
Applications will be reviewed according to the Firm’s recruitment needs, and all candidates are assessed on merit against the requirements of the role. Only shortlisted candidates may be contacted.

## Applicant Privacy
Information submitted in connection with recruitment will be used to assess applications and manage the recruitment process. CVs submitted for an advertised position will normally be retained for up to 90 days following the relevant recruitment process, and general applications for up to 90 days from the date of receipt, unless a longer period is required by law or separately agreed with the applicant. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for further information.
