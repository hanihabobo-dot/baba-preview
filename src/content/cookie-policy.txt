# Cookie Policy
~ Clear Information. Informed Choices.
Effective from the date this website is first published.
This Cookie Policy explains how Baba Law Firm uses cookies and similar technologies on its website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

## What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on a device when a website is visited. Similar technologies may also be used to support website functionality, remember preferences, measure performance or protect the site against misuse.

## Cookies We May Use

### Strictly Necessary Cookies
These support essential functions such as website security, form submission, session management and the recording of privacy preferences. They are used where necessary for the website to operate.

### Analytics Cookies
If analytics are enabled, these may help us understand aggregate website traffic and how visitors use the site. Non-essential analytics cookies will be activated only where permitted and after any required consent has been obtained.

### Third-Party and Security Technologies
Our website may use third-party services for hosting, security, spam prevention or embedded content. Depending on the service and configuration, these providers may place cookies or process technical information.

## Managing Your Preferences
Where optional cookies are used, visitors will be able to accept or reject them through the cookie banner and revisit their choices using Cookie Settings →. You may also restrict or delete cookies through your browser settings, although disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

## Cookie Register
The cookies and similar technologies currently used on this website, together with their provider, purpose, category and duration, are listed in the Cookie Settings available on this website.

## Updates and Contact
We may update this Policy when the website’s technology or relevant requirements change. For questions, contact info@babalawfirm.net.
