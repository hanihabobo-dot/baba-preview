# Our Firm
~ Judicial Experience. Commercial Understanding.
Baba Law Firm is an independent Syrian law firm based in Damascus, advising businesses, investors and institutions on corporate, commercial, regulatory and dispute resolution matters.
Our roots in the Syrian legal profession date back to 1967. Our background is distinguished by a combination of judicial experience, commercial arbitration and legal practice, particularly in matters involving public authorities and the Syrian legal system.
From the judicial and arbitral career of Tawfeeq Baba, former President of the Syrian Council of State, to the legal practice and academic work of Managing Partner Dr. Mohamad Baba, the firm draws on two generations of experience in addressing the relationship between commercial disputes, arbitration, national courts and public law.
Today, we bring this understanding to the legal needs of businesses and investors operating in Syria.

## Our Heritage
~ Experience Across the Judiciary, Arbitration and Legal Practice.
Our story began in Aleppo in 1967, when Tawfeeq Baba founded the firm.
His subsequent judicial career included senior responsibilities within the Syrian judiciary and culminated in his appointment as President of the Syrian Council of State.
His professional experience also includes serving as presiding arbitrator and chair of arbitral tribunals in numerous commercial disputes involving foreign companies, particularly those arising from supply agreements and contracts with Syrian government entities.
Following his retirement from the judiciary in 2012, Tawfeeq Baba returned to legal practice and continues to contribute his judicial and arbitral experience to Baba Law Firm as Senior Counsel.

## Our Leadership
~ Building on Experience. Advancing Legal Practice.
Baba Law Firm is led by Dr. Mohamad Baba, Managing Partner, whose legal practice focuses on corporate and commercial law, foreign investment, banking and finance, arbitration and dispute resolution.
Dr. Baba holds a PhD in Private Law, with doctoral research entitled *The Role of National Courts in Arbitration Matters*. He also holds a Master’s degree in Private Law, with research entitled *Arbitration in Administrative Contracts under Syrian Law*, and an LL.M. in Banking, Corporate and Finance Law from Fordham University School of Law in New York.

The firm’s specialised legal team brings experience across corporate and commercial matters, regulatory issues, litigation and dispute resolution, providing clients with coordinated legal advice tailored to their needs.
