1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Baba Law Firm, an independent Syrian law firm.

Privacy contact: info@babalawfirm.net · +963 11 666 4221

2. Information We May Collect

  • Your name
  • Organisation or company
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Information contained in an enquiry or message
  • Any other information you choose to provide
  • Recruitment information, including CVs, employment and education history, and information submitted through the Careers application form
  • Limited technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited and general website-usage information, depending on the technologies implemented.

3. How We Use Personal Information

  • Respond to enquiries and communications
  • Assess whether and how we may assist with a legal matter
  • Communicate with prospective or existing clients
  • Receive, assess and respond to employment and internship applications
  • Operate, maintain and secure the website
  • Understand and improve website performance and usage
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory or professional obligations
  • Protect the rights, security and legitimate interests of Baba Law Firm and others

4. Confidential Information

Submitting information through this website does not create a lawyer-client relationship. Please do not submit confidential, privileged or highly sensitive information through the website or its contact forms unless and until Baba Law Firm has agreed to act for you and appropriate communication arrangements have been established.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Personal information may be shared where reasonably necessary with service providers supporting the operation, hosting, security or maintenance of the website, subject to appropriate arrangements where required. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, court order or competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or protect legal rights.

The website currently uses the following service providers:

Cloudflare
Cloudflare provides content delivery and security services for the website. It processes technical information, such as IP addresses, to protect the website against malicious traffic, and may set strictly necessary security cookies.
Google reCAPTCHA
Google reCAPTCHA protects the contact and Careers forms against spam. It assesses technical information about your device and your interaction with the page.
Google Analytics
With your consent, we use Google Analytics, through Site Kit by Google, to understand how visitors use the website. It is not loaded unless you allow analytics cookies, and you can change your choice at any time through Cookie Settings at the foot of every page.
Google Maps
The map on the Contact page is provided by Google Maps. It loads only when you choose to load it or have allowed maps in your cookie settings; Google then receives technical information such as your IP address.

6. International Processing

Some website, hosting, email, security or technology service providers may process or store information outside Syria. Where this occurs, we seek to use service providers and arrangements appropriate to the nature of the information and the services being provided.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information handled through the website against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or electronic storage system, however, can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention

We retain personal information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, assessing recruitment applications, maintaining appropriate business records and complying with legal or professional obligations. Website enquiries and recruitment applications are generally retained for up to 90 days, unless a longer period is required for an ongoing client relationship, recruitment process, legal obligation or legitimate operational purpose.

Enquiries sent through the contact form are delivered by email to info@babalawfirm.net, and applications sent through the Careers form are delivered by email to careers@babalawfirm.net, rather than being stored in the website’s database.

9. Your Information

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the request, you may contact us to ask about personal information we hold concerning you or to request that inaccurate information be corrected. Other rights or requests concerning personal information may apply under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies and similar technologies necessary for its operation and, where implemented, to understand website usage and performance. Further information is available in our Cookie Policy.

11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, practices, technology or applicable legal requirements. The date shown at the top of this page indicates when the Policy was last updated.
