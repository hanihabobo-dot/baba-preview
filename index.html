Syrian Law. International Perspective.

Baba Law Firm is an independent Syrian law firm based in Damascus, advising businesses, investors and institutions on corporate, commercial, regulatory and dispute resolution matters.

From entering the Syrian market to completing a transaction or resolving a dispute, we provide clear legal advice grounded in Syrian law and informed by our clients’ commercial objectives.

At a Glance

The Firm in Brief.

  • Established in 1967 — A heritage spanning generations in the Syrian legal profession.
  • Based in Damascus — Advising and representing clients across Syria.
  • 25 Lawyers — A team bringing together transactional, regulatory and dispute resolution experience.
  • Three Languages — Working with clients in Arabic, English and French.

A Market Opening to Investment

Local Understanding for International Business.

As Syria’s economy reopens to international trade and investment, opportunities require careful assessment of the legal framework, regulatory requirements and practical conditions for doing business.

We help clients evaluate their options, establish an appropriate presence, structure investments and commercial relationships, and address the legal issues that arise as their activities develop.

How We Help

Legal Support at Every Stage.

  • 01Entering the Market — Preparing market and feasibility studies, assessing market-entry options, establishing companies and foreign branches, structuring ownership and obtaining licences and regulatory approvals.
  • 02Operating in Syria — Supporting commercial contracts, corporate governance, employment, banking arrangements, taxation and ongoing regulatory compliance.
  • 03Investing and Growing — Structuring acquisitions, joint ventures, financing, real estate developments, and construction and infrastructure projects.
  • 04Resolving Disputes — Protecting clients’ interests through negotiation, arbitration, commercial litigation, enforcement and proceedings before the Syrian Council of State.
