What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can be used to enable website functionality, remember preferences, maintain security and understand how visitors use a website.

How We Use Cookies

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function properly or securely and cannot generally be disabled through our website. They may be used for functions such as security, network management and maintaining user preferences.

Analytics Cookies

If enabled, analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website, such as which pages are visited and how the website is used. This information is used to evaluate and improve website performance.

Preference Cookies

These cookies may remember choices made by visitors, such as cookie preferences or other website settings.
